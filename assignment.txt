Place take-home exercise
Hi Person,
Thanks for taking the time to do this. The exercise below is meant to resemble the kind of work the team does day
to day, and we expect you to use Claude (or any other tools you normally use) the way you would on the job. Since
Claude can take care of much of the typing, we're especially interested in the decisions you make and how well the
result holds up.
Background
Our product is a field sensor that reports its position to a mobile app while a crew works. The sensor streams data
to the app continuously, and the app shows the crew what the sensor is doing. Things go wrong on both sides in
the field, and the app has to notice.
The task
Build a small system with two parts, in any language (or combination of languages) you like:
A server that plays the role of the sensor. It generates semi-realistic location data and streams it to the client
using a simple messaging protocol of your own design. It should be able to produce valid data and also inject
faults.
A client that plays the role of the app. It receives the stream, shows the sensor's status, and detects faults.
The interface can be anything: a web page, a desktop app, or a console.
The client should also be able to send commands to the sensor, for example to change how often it reports or to
start and stop a fault.
What to send back
Your code, in a zip file or a link to a repository, with instructions for running it.
A short description of your protocol, written so that someone else could build a compatible client without
reading your code.
A short note on how well your client detects faults, and how you know.
A short note on any assumptions you made.
The transcript of your Claude (or alternative tool) conversation or conversations. We want to see how you
worked with your AI tools, not to grade how your prompts are worded.
If you have time
These are optional. Pick any that interest you, or none.
Show the sensor's position on a map.
Record a session and replay it later through the client.
Something you'd want if you were the one using it.
Time
Please spend no more than three hours of working time in total. It doesn't need to be done in one sitting, so feel free
to stop and come back to it. If you run out of time, stop and include a few lines on what you would do next. An
incomplete result with a clear account of where it stands is more useful to us than a complete one that took all
weekend.
Logistics
Please send your submission to name@place.com by Friday, October 23. Feel free to email me at the
same address with any questions or anything you'd like clarified, or if you run into a problem. You can reach me
anytime, any day, and the best way to reach me is to text me at ###-###-####. Time spent waiting to hear back
from me doesn't count toward the three hours.
After we receive your submission, we'll set up a follow-up discussion where you can walk us through it and talk
about how you'd extend it.
Thanks, and good luck.
